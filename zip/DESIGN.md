## Brand & Style

Navano Sovereign Governance balances institutional authority, military-grade cryptographic security, and refined executive minimalism. Engineered for high-stakes decision makers, venture partners, and protocol custodians, the aesthetic conveys precision, cryptographic trust, and sovereign control over institutional assets.

The visual style blends **Technical Minimalism** with **Sovereign Dark Glassmorphism**:
- Deep obsidian and charcoal foundation surfaces accented by subtle architectural grid patterns (`32px x 32px`).
- Electric cobalt and icy cyan accents signaling verified integrity, active telemetry, and high-tier sovereign standing.
- Ultra-sharp typography pairing monospace accents with technical geometric headings and humanist functional body copy.
- Translucent backdrop blur planes layered over restrained radial glows to construct atmospheric depth without sacrificing legible data density.

## Colors

The palette is engineered specifically for low-fatigue sovereign operations in dark mode. 

- **Primary (`#adc6ff` / `#4c8eff`):** Represents verified state indicators, active cryptographic sessions, and primary interaction triggers.
- **Surface Elevation Hierarchy:** Built on five tonal steps ranging from deep void `#0b0f10` (container lowest) through `#101415` (canvas background), up to `#323537` (container highest) for hovering elements and elevated inputs.
- **Outlines (`#414754` variant, `#8c909f` standard):** Provide hairline boundaries with low-opacity thresholds (`20%` to `40%`) to retain structure against the dark grid backdrop.
- **Semantic Feedback:** Critical actions and termination hooks utilize `#ffb4ab` with deep wine containers (`#93000a`), while verified hardware tokens glow with primary tints.

## Typography

The typographic system uses a tripartite hierarchy designed for technical command centers:

1. **Space Grotesk (Headlines):** Delivers an architectural, futuristic feel for institutional identity titles, hero section labels, and modal headings.
2. **Manrope (Body):** Ensures sustained reading comfort, natural optical balance, and effortless comprehension in dense data forms and mandate text.
3. **Geist (Labels & Telemetry):** Provides clean tabular numerals and high-tracking uppercase labels for system identifiers, hardware specs, timestamps, and compliance badges.

## Layout & Spacing

The architecture operates on a responsive 12-column grid capped at a maximum width of `1400px` for optimal viewing on large displays.

- **Desktop Layout:** Fixed-width, persistent executive navigation rail (`288px` / `w-72`) pinned alongside an elastic 12-column canvas (split into an 8-column primary dossier area and a 4-column telemetry rail).
- **Responsive Adaptations:**
  - **Mobile (< 768px):** Navigation transitions from a docked sidebar to an off-canvas drawer. The dossier grid stacks vertically into a single column with collapsed `1rem` margins.
  - **Tablet (768px - 1024px):** Sidebar collapses into an icon rail, and metrics transition into a 2x2 grid.
  - **Desktop (>= 1024px):** Full multi-pane view with `1.5rem` to `2.5rem` internal component rhythm.
- **Rhythm:** Spacing increments adhere strictly to a 4px/8px baseline grid (`space-xs` = 4px, `space-sm` = 8px, `space-md` = 16px, `space-lg` = 24px, `space-xl` = 40px).

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is established through translucent stacking, frosted glass surfaces, and subtle ambient shadows rather than harsh physical skeuomorphism:

- **Surface Translucency:** Cards utilize `surface-container-low` with a 70–80% alpha mask coupled with `backdrop-blur-md` (`12px` to `16px`), allowing the background grid (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.02)`) to create depth.
- **Hairline Outlines:** Panels are contained by thin 1px borders of `outline-variant/30` or `outline-variant/40`, generating crisp boundary definitions.
- **Ambient Floor Glows:** Modals and focal panels cast low-luminosity, extra-diffuse shadows (`shadow-2xl shadow-surface-container-lowest/60`).
- **Active State Highlights:** Active navigation segments and primary focus states project an electric radial glow (`shadow-lg shadow-primary/20`).

## Shapes

The design uses a restrained, semi-square geometry (`roundedness: 1`):

- **Standard Elements (DEFAULT):** `0.125rem` (2px) for sharp status tags and micro badges.
- **Interactive Controls (lg):** `0.25rem` (4px) for input fields, inline buttons, and key pills.
- **Cards & Enclosures (xl):** `0.5rem` (8px) to `0.75rem` (12px) for high-level dossier sections, identity containers, and session modals.
- **Avatars & Realtime Pings:** Circular pills (`rounded-full`) reserved strictly for user monograms, active telemetry pulses, and status pills.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Solid `bg-primary` with `text-on-primary`, subtle outer drop glow (`shadow-lg shadow-primary/20`), active scale bounce (`active:scale-[0.98]`), and paired inline hotkey kbd caps.
- **Secondary / Ghost:** `bg-surface-container-high` with `border border-outline-variant/50`, `text-on-surface`, transitioning to `bg-surface-variant` on hover.
- **Destructive:** Borderless or ghost styling utilizing `text-error` hovering to `bg-error-container/20`.

### Form Fields & Inputs
- **Base Structure:** `bg-surface-container-lowest/80` bordered by `border-outline-variant/40` with `rounded-lg`.
- **Text & Placeholder:** Medium-weight text in `text-on-surface`, placeholder rendered in `text-outline`.
- **Focus State:** 1px outline ring colored `primary/40` with instant transition.
- **Prefix / Suffix Elements:** Technical handles (such as `@` and dialing codes) isolated in `bg-surface-container` blocks.

### Status Chips & Badges
- **Verified Status:** High-contrast pill featuring a pulsating live CSS beacon (`animate-ping`), uppercase `label-sm` font, and `bg-surface-container` casing with `border-outline-variant/40`.
- **Classification Badges:** Monospace micro-badges (`#NVN-KHAN-01`, `FIPS-140-3`) set in `Geist` with muted surface backing.

### Dossier & Information Cards
- Segmented internal headers with lower 1px border dividers (`border-outline-variant/20`).
- Divided summary strips featuring multi-column data layouts (`divide-x divide-outline-variant/30`).
- Top-level hero cards incorporate atmospheric ambient top gradients with watermark identity typography.

### Hardware & Session List Items
- Compact rows featuring rounded leading icon slots (`bg-surface-container-highest` or `primary-container/20`), dual-line metadata stacks (primary descriptor + monospace status subtext), and trailing sync indicators.
